Ronnie Vogel

From go-karts to supercars, and then to running race teams.

Ronnie Vogel grew up around race cars and go-karts in Miami. As Vice President and Director of Motorsports at Ferrari of Fort Lauderdale he ran its racing programmes and managed the Squadra Corse team.

“I grew up around race cars, watching the Miami Grand Prix and hanging out with the drivers.”

Ronnie Vogel · Maxim, 2019
