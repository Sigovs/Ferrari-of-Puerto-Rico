Challenge overview

The Passione Ferrari Club Challenge is a driving program inspired by the Ferrari Challenge series.

It is dedicated to Ferrari Challenge Car owners who want to enjoy the thrill of driving, or wish to develop their skills to eventually become involved in racing.

1The Club

Exclusive club

Ferrari invites you to join us at six legendary circuits across Europe, where you’ll compete with fellow Ferrari Challenge members in a truly unique racing competition. This is an opportunity to live, breathe, and share your passion for Ferrari and for racing.

This exclusive track experience is available only to Passione Ferrari Club Challenge members. The membership is valid for 3 years, and all new members will receive a personalised set of racing gear.

Full-season package* — 6 rounds

  • France, Le Castellet
  • Italy, Mugello
  • Austria, Spielberg
  • Portugal, Portimão
  • Italy, Imola — Day 1
  • Italy, Imola — Day 2

Individual rounds

Each package includes dedicated track time and full hospitality for two people, with breakfast, lunch, and a closing aperitif. Each car can be shared by a maximum of two drivers at each event, allowing you to enjoy the experience with friends and family.

*The Finali Mondiali round is not included in the full-season package.

The cars

All historic Ferrari Challenge cars will be welcome in this program. All cars must be Ferrari Genuine with no modifications made.

2Format and activities

Format and activities

Passione Ferrari Club Challenge membership includes:

Dedicated Driving Slots

Endurance and sprint driving sessions divided during the day (up to 4 hours).

Contact your Official Ferrari dealer for further information and reservations.

Personal Ferrari instructors

One-to-one coaching Optional Ferrari professional instructors are available for one-on-one coaching (upon request).

Professional Driver Tutoring Complimentary light tutoring from a Ferrari professional instructor will be provided during each event.

Contact your Official Ferrari dealer for further information and reservations.

Telemetry Analysis

Conducted by our expert technicians, drivers can analyse data to enhance skills and performance on the track.

Contact your Official Ferrari dealer for further information and reservations.

Lounge area

Access to an exclusive hospitality area where members and their guests can relax and enjoy unique benefits, including breakfast, lunch, and a closing aperitif.

Contact your Official Ferrari dealer for further information and reservations.

Closing Ceremony & Guest Activities

Closing Ceremony* Celebrate the season with all other drivers from each round of the season.

Guest Activities Dedicated activities complement the on-track action, ensuring your guests feel warmly welcomed into the world of Ferrari.

* The closing ceremony will take place during the Imola round.

Each car can be shared by a maximum of two drivers at each event, allowing you to enjoy the experience with friends and family.

3Hospitality

Lounge area

Situated at the heart of each event, these exclusive members-only areas offer a comfortable space for you and your guest to relax and enjoy the circuit activities. A gourmet Italian lunch will be served during breaks between sessions.

The Passione Ferrari Club Challenge lounge areas include:

  • Private lounge and restaurant

  • All-day refreshments

4Photo and MyFerrari App

Exclusive memories just for you

Our official photographer and videographer will be at every event to capture the memories of your best laps, along with those special moments off the track to be cherished for years to come. After each event, photos will be available for download from the private members-only website, along with a highlights video of each event.

The Challenge at Your Fingertips with the MyFerrari App

The MyFerrari app features a dedicated area for Passione Ferrari Club Challenge members, offering:

  • Real-time event itineraries and live track updates to keep you informed.
  • The latest track information and on-board videos to help you familiarise yourself with each circuit.
  • Access to your lap times and telemetry data, allowing you to track your progress and performance at each event.
  • The ability to download professional photos and videos from each event.
  • Opportunities to connect with fellow Club members to further enhance experience.
