Discover service contracts and maintenance services for the entire Ferrari range.

Warranty3 years

Manufacturer’s Warranty

Every new Ferrari vehicle comes with a standard three year-unlimited mileage express limited warranty, which allows the owner to enjoy the driving experience without worrying about the unexpected.

The Ferrari Manufacturer’s Warranty provides coverage for replacement parts and labor. The manufacturer warranty gives owners peace of mind from the very first day of ownership.

Maintenance7 years

7-year Genuine Maintenance

The scheduled routine maintenance plan offered for the first 7 years of life of all Ferrari cars is called Genuine Maintenance.

It covers regular scheduled maintenance, original spare parts, and checks carried out meticulously by professional technicians trained at the Ferrari Training Centre in Maranello. The maintenance history is updated in the Ferrari database after every service, with the option of obtaining a printed copy of the digital record. The service is available for the entire Ferrari range, including new cars and pre-owned Ferraris purchased at an Official Dealer.

The program offers two maintenance options that can be adapted to different driving requirements: (i) every 12,500 miles or (ii) once a year without mileage restrictions. This flexibility allows the service to be personalized for both occasional and regular Ferrari drivers. Maintaining a complete and up-to-date maintenance history maximizes the value of the car.

This service is available in all markets worldwide and from all dealerships in the Official Dealership Network. (Note: these conditions may not apply to limited-edition or one-off models.)

Years4 to 8

Ferrari Service Contract

The Ferrari Manufacturer’s Warranty can be followed by additional service contracts renewable up to the 8th year of the car’s service life, maintaining the highest levels of protection and including roadside assistance service.

Ferrari Service Contract can be purchased for an additional price at the time of vehicle purchase, at the expiration of the Manufacturer’s Warranty, or even later, and can be extended in increments of one year.

Duration

The Ferrari service contracts can be purchased for periods of 12 months at a time, with the option of renewing annually, from the 4th to the 8th year of the car’s service life.

Requirements

To qualify for the program the car must:

  • be inspected by an authorized Ferrari dealer to certify compliance with the quality prerequisites specified by Ferrari and verify the provenance of the maintenance history.
  • not exceed a mileage of 75,000 miles.
  • not have been involved in serious accidents, tampered with, or used in competitions.
  • not have been involved in track activities or for commercial purposes.

Hybrid rangeAll PHEV models

Ferrari Service Contract Hybrid

Ferrari plug-in hybrid models benefit from additional service contracts, including replacement of the vehicle battery after the 8th year and it’s state of health lower than a specific percentage.

Ferrari ensures the use of the latest technology, maintaining peak-level performance and reliability for all your cars. Please contact your dealer to learn more.

Coverage

The Ferrari Service Contract Hybrid program is dedicated to Ferrari plug-in hybrid vehicle (PHEV) owners. This service preserves the performance and excellence of Ferrari sports cars. It also includes scheduled high-voltage battery (HVB) replacement after the 8th year of the vehicle’s service life, at no additional cost, provided the client maintains continuous service contract coverage. In the event of future technological battery developments, the replacement HVB will be a new component that allows the car’s performance to be maintained.

Duration

The program offers renewable packages (annual ones), irrespective of the car’s year of production.

Requirements

The program is applicable to all Ferrari plug-in hybrid vehicles (PHEVs). The program can also be activated on cars outside of their factory warranty, subject to technical inspection by Ferrari personnel. It can also be transferred to subsequent owners of the car in the event of a transfer of ownership.

Ferrari Power Contract + Maintenance

The Ferrari Power Contract + Maintenance program combines the advantages of the Ferrari power Contract with the peace of mind that comes from regular vehicle maintenance.

This complete Ferrari package allows you to enjoy the benefits of Ferrari Power Contract and extends the original maintenance program of the vehicle at the same time. The service includes special complementary maintenance and Roadside Assistance. The Service Contract remains valid even if the car is sold, as long as the specific quality Ferrari requirements noted below are met.

Supercar, Icona and past models

SupercarLaFerrari

Ferrari Service Contract LaFerrari + Maintenance

Combining routine scheduled maintenance and service contract in a single solution, Ferrari Service Contract LaFerrari + Maintenance provides owners with the ultimate carefree driving experience.

It represents the peak of Ferrari aftersales programs, ensuring the functionality of the vehicle with service contract that extends to the entire car, excluding defects resulting from normal wear and tear. The service contract also includes coverage of all the hybrid components of the car.

Duration

This service is guaranteed for 12 months with the option to renew it up to year 16th.

Requirements

The service is specifically designed for owners of LaFerrari and LaFerrari Aperta models. To activate and maintain the service, the vehicle must pass a dedicated technical test and comply with parameters of the specific checklist for the model. Maintenance must have been performed in accordance with the program specified by Ferrari and the mileage must not exceed 56,000 miles. The vehicle must not have been repaired or modified inappropriately or by unauthorized centers, and the use must comply with Ferrari specifications. Activation is subject to approval by Ferrari, which reserves the right to verify the technical eligibility of the vehicle. The service can be activated from two months before the expiration of the standard manufacturer’s warranty up to the 12th year of the vehicle’s service life. The coverage is valid only at Ferrari Authorized Network centers. The maintenance history must be sent to Ferrari to complete the digital service records.
