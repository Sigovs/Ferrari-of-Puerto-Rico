Timeless excitement

Club Competizioni GT embodies the essence of Ferrari’s rich racing legacy, blending over thirty years of history with the stars of today’s premier GT championships.

This exclusive initiative celebrates the most captivating and decorated Ferrari GT race cars from recent decades. Owners of these remarkable vehicles, produced since 1989, have the unique opportunity to participate in events held at some of the world’s most iconic and prestigious circuits. It’s a rare chance to bring Ferrari’s racing heritage to life on track, showcasing the legendary cars that have contributed to the marque’s storied list of achievements.

2The Club

A new definition of club

Club Competizioni GT provides owners of eligible GT racing cars with the exclusive opportunity to become part of a distinguished group, where they can share their passion for the Ferrari brand on the track.

It offers the rare chance to drive their own car in a completely safe racing environment, with the guidance and expert advice of official Ferrari Endurance drivers. The experience delivers unmatched thrills, both on and off the track, offering the kind of excitement that only Ferrari can offer.

In this world, the customer is at the center, enjoying a wide range of bespoke services, including private sessions with a dedicated professional driver.

3Cars

Eligible models

Thirteen Ferrari models are eligible to join the Club Competizioni GT, ranging from the F40 Competizione of 1989 to the newest 296 GT3s currently available to Ferrari’s racing customers. In addition to the basic equipment provided by club membership, which includes a custom clothing kit and a set of tyres for each round, there are also special on demand services such as exclusive sessions with a professional driver, car transport, customised logistics, and the option of a tailored menu in hospitality.

New frontiers, more fun

In response to the evolving needs of its customers, Ferrari has expanded its Club Competizioni GT events by introducing a dedicated US programme, in addition to the global one. This programme offer participants the chance to enjoy extra track days, while still benefiting from the exclusive on-track support services. New dates, new opportunities, and unparalleled thrills await.
